\title{One Operator Is Not Enough --- Yet: The Structural Cost of a
Single-Operator Mathematical Representation, Measured at Fixed Scale
with Published Nulls}
\author{Anonymous ACL submission}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Odrzywo{\l}ek~\cite{odrzywolek2026} showed that every elementary function is
constructible from the constant~$1$ and a single binary operator,
$\eml(x,y)=\exp(x)-\ln(y)$, making any expression a strict binary tree whose
internal nodes all carry the same operation. A single-operator representation is
maximally regular, which is attractive for graph learning; the obvious cost is
size. Before asking whether the regularity helps a model, we measure the cost
exactly, at one fixed scale: a deterministic, quality-audited corpus of
$250{,}000$ expressions with frozen splits. Compiling the corpus with the pinned
official compiler yields a median tree expansion factor $\alpha=40.66$ over the
source AST (mean $952.1$, p99 $10{,}448.6$); $0/250{,}000$ expressions pass the
preregistered per-family thresholds ($1.29$--$1.50$; the whole-grammar counting
break-even is $\approx\!1.56$). Lossless DAG sharing compresses pure-EML trees
$39.4\times$ on average, yet the shared EML DAG is larger than the
\emph{uncompressed} AST tree for every one of the $250{,}000$ expressions (best
remaining ratio exactly $8/7$). Bounded, verified equality saturation improves
$23.9$--$27.6\%$ of costed expressions at $2.4$--$2.8\%$ mean relative savings,
conditional on $60.7\%$ vocabulary coverage. We publish two preregistered nulls
--- a learned motif vocabulary loses to an equal-budget frequent baseline, and a
neural cost ranker loses to a structural heuristic --- and one preregistered gate
verdict, a \emph{fail}, reported with content hashes rather than softened. We
also release a frozen, leakage-controlled learning apparatus (equivalence,
rewrite-step, proof/simplification, and symbolic-regression tracks) whose
training verdicts are the paper's only pending numbers; the pair pipeline has
already run end-to-end on a byte-identical pilot corpus. The study estimates no
scaling laws, and frontier LLMs appear only as external context.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A single-operator representation of mathematics is maximally regular: a graph
network reading a pure-EML term never has to learn \emph{what} an operator is,
only \emph{where} things connect. The obvious objection is size --- lowering even
a small trigonometric expression such as $\sin(x)$ to pure EML produces hundreds
of nodes --- and the standard reply is that sharing (DAGs), macros, and semantic
rewriting could close the gap. Whether they actually do is an empirical question
that, to our knowledge, had not been measured exactly at a nontrivial scale. The
compression program exists to close that gap; the learning program asks whether
whatever gap remains is worth paying for as inductive bias.

This paper is deliberately narrow. It is a \emph{structural feasibility study}
under preregistered thresholds and complete denominators, publishing null and
failed outcomes alongside positives, and it separates the two questions cleanly:
what the representation \emph{costs} structurally (which we measure, in full, at
production scale) from whether that cost \emph{buys} anything for learning (which
we do not yet answer, and do not pretend to). We fit no scaling law, run no
leaderboard, and use no frontier LLM as a controlled baseline. Every empirical
sentence carries a claim-register identifier, and every number below matches a
committed per-goal summary exactly.

Contributions, each tied to a claim-register entry:
\textbf{C1}, a deterministic quality-audited $250{,}000$-expression corpus with
frozen splits and checksummed manifests (\S\ref{sec:data});
\textbf{C2}, the exact expansion result --- median $\alpha=40.66$, $0/250{,}000$
preregistered threshold passes (\S\ref{sec:results});
\textbf{C3}, exact lossless DAG sharing --- $39.4\times$ mean EML
self-compression, AST parity on zero expressions (\S\ref{sec:results});
\textbf{C4}, verified e-graph rewriting~\cite{willsey2021egg} and motif
compression under honest denominators, including two preregistered nulls
(\S\ref{sec:results});
\textbf{C9}, a bounded compiler-conformance audit whose preregistered gate
verdict is a published \textbf{fail}, with artifact content hashes
(\S\ref{sec:gate}); and a frozen, CI-tested, leakage-controlled learning
apparatus (\S\S\ref{sec:models}--\ref{sec:tasks}), whose training verdicts
(C5--C8, C10--C11) are the paper's only pending numbers and are reported as
\texttt{insufficient\_evidence} until the runs land.

\section{Representations, immutable data, and hypotheses}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Representations}
We compare a fixed family of representations, all derived from the same source
expression: the ordered source AST as a tree and as an exact hash-consed DAG; the
official-v4 pure-EML tree and DAG; transparent macro and motif-aware graphs
(macro-derived compression vocabularies, never described as single-operator EML);
a prefix-sequence control; and a transparent feature-count floor. Every graph
preserves roots, ordered child slots, edge directions and roles, repeated
references, exact values, and representation identity. Structural identity is
kept strictly separate from semantic equivalence: two expressions share structure
only when their canonical ordered signatures match, and equivalence is a distinct,
verifier-established relation used only where we say so.

\subsection{Fixed data foundation (C1)}
\label{sec:corpus}
The corpus contains $250{,}000$ unique expressions with splits of exactly
$175{,}000\,/\,25{,}000\,/\,25{,}000\,/\,25{,}000$
(train\,/\,validation\,/\,test\_iid\,/\,test\_ood) and six source families with
exact quotas: algebraic core ($70{,}000$), exp/log ($40{,}000$),
powers/division/rationals ($40{,}000$), trig/hyperbolic ($40{,}000$), mixed
elementary ($35{,}000$), and an OOD-stress family
($25{,}000$).\anchor{docs/goals/goal2/GOAL2\_SUMMARY.md, ``Corpus and
denominators''; FINAL\_REPORT.md \#c1-goals-1-5-corpus (pre-authentication digest
index).} Generation is deterministic and QA-gated; manifests carry per-shard
checksums, the config hash, seed, and clean commit. The \texttt{ood\_stress} view
is a stored combined stress profile over size, depth, variable count, and
composition, and is never relabeled strict depth-OOD. Corpus v2/v3 and any
regeneration of the Goals~1--5 inputs are deliberately deferred: this study is
fixed to the immutable v1 foundation.

\subsection{Predeclared outcome families}
Outcome families were declared before the production runs and are never collapsed
into one scalar leaderboard. They are: \emph{structural} (tree and DAG size and
depth, pure-EML $\alpha$ where compatible, macro size, dictionary-inclusive motif
MDL); \emph{predictive} (six-arm equivalence metrics); \emph{rewrite/proof}
(action and exact-successor match, verifier-valid safety, exact-target proof,
simplification cost); \emph{SR} (exact and verifier-confirmed recovery); and
\emph{compiler-v2 conformance} only. Per-family expansion thresholds were fixed
in advance. Structural statistics below use all $250{,}000$ rows; semantic
auditing is a separate bounded sample (\S\ref{sec:limits}).

\section{Compact models and fair compute}
\label{sec:models}

This section and \S\ref{sec:tasks} describe committed, CI-tested machinery whose
production training has not been run; no number in them is a result. We describe
the apparatus because it is frozen and because its design --- not its yet-unrun
output --- is a contribution.

\subsection{A compact GINE-style graph encoder}
The four graph arms share one project-specific, compact edge-aware GINE-style
encoder~\cite{xu2019powerful,hu2020strategies} --- not an exact reproduction of
any prior architecture. It has three edge-aware message-passing layers; hidden
width $64$ or $96$, frozen once by measured parameter/FLOP matching; roughly
$0.2$--$1.0$ million total task parameters; node-kind, node-label, and
exact-value category/encoding embeddings; edge-direction, edge-role, and
ordered-slot embeddings; residual connections, GraphNorm or LayerNorm, dropout, a
compact two-layer FFN, and sum pooling; and an optional virtual node whose setting
is frozen before any test evaluation. For a message edge $u\rightarrow v$ the
edge feature and update are
\begin{equation}
a_{uv}=E_{\mathrm{dir}}(d_{uv})+E_{\mathrm{slot}}(s_{uv})+E_{\mathrm{role}}(r_{uv}),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde h_v^{(\ell+1)}
&=\operatorname{MLP}_{\ell}\big((1+\epsilon_\ell)\,h_v^{(\ell)}+m_v^{(\ell)}\big),\\
m_v^{(\ell)}&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal N(v)}
\operatorname{ReLU}\big(h_u^{(\ell)}+W_\ell\,a_{uv}\big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with the residual/normalization/dropout/FFN ordering fixed by the frozen
implementation. The four arms differ only by input channel: source AST-DAG,
pure-EML-DAG, an honestly labeled macro-derived frequent-motif DAG, and a
train-only motif-AST fair control capped by both the reference vocabulary entry
limit and exact dictionary MDL bits, with every compressed graph required to
reconstruct.

\subsection{Task composition and controls}
For symmetric equivalence classification, shared endpoint encoders produce graph
embeddings $g_a,g_b$ combined as
\begin{equation}
p(a,b)=\big[\,g_a+g_b,\ \lvert g_a-g_b\rvert,\ g_a\odot g_b\,\big],
\label{eq:pair}
\end{equation}
which is symmetric in $a,b$ by construction; we report the swap-invariance test
rather than assuming it. Rewrite-policy, value, and proof heads are directional
and instead use goal-conditioned, order-sensitive features. The prefix
transformer is a real matched control over a prefix token sequence, not a straw
man. The transparent floor uses exactly $19$ predeclared surface counts ---
operator, primitive, variable, size, and depth counts --- that every learned arm
must beat; we state explicitly that representation-specific vocabulary embeddings
count toward the matched parameter budget, because pure-EML label vocabularies
differ per channel and move the totals.

\subsection{Compute matching and training}
\label{sec:compute}
Because pure-EML graphs are much larger than AST or macro graphs for the same
expression, equal parameters are \emph{not} equal compute; we therefore report,
jointly with every quality number, the full compute envelope: parameters,
measured and estimated FLOPs under labeled methods, wall time, peak host and
device memory, GPU-hours, graph/token size, effective examples and nodes per
update, node/edge batch budget, gradient accumulation, the optimizer-step and
example budget, optimizer, early-stopping state, precision, and deterministic
settings. Hidden width and the virtual-node flag are frozen only by a measured
parameter/FLOP preflight on production vocabularies, which do not exist yet, so
the grid config records \texttt{pending\_preflight\_freeze} rather than a guess.
Table~\ref{tab:params} gives the current fixture-vocabulary measurements that
bound the freeze. We use seeds \texttt{20260726}, \texttt{20260727}, and
\texttt{20260728} and publish every seed row; the mean never replaces the raw
rows. The default production profile is $2\times$H100 $80$\,GB, with $4\times$H100
used only after a measured throughput pilot justifies it.

\section{Bounded tasks and verification}
\label{sec:tasks}

The learning tracks are bounded and verifier-gated, and their datasets are frozen
or in a documented, reproducible build state. Missing cells are explicit missing
states, never zeros.

\subsection{Equivalence}
The equivalence track freezes exactly $50{,}000/5{,}000/5{,}000/5{,}000$ base pair
records over the four aligned graph channels plus the sequence and floor controls.
Source-, e-class-, and trace-relative grouping prevents leakage; IID and OOD views
are reported without changing base totals. The pair pipeline has already run
end-to-end on a byte-identical $10{,}000$-row pilot corpus, binding real providers
(a rule engine and the replay verifier
\texttt{geml-goal6-rule-\allowbreak engine-\allowbreak replay-\allowbreak verifier-v1}): $9{,}039$ accepted records in
$34$ CPU minutes --- $6{,}867$ replay-verified positives and $2{,}172$
exact-rationally refuted negatives --- with $2{,}824$ rejections and $3{,}133$
failures retained under typed reasons, and the shortfall against the frozen
production targets reported explicitly as \texttt{complete\_short} rather than
resampled away (Table~\ref{tab:pilot}). One honest quirk is retained rather than
patched: \texttt{test\_ood} yielded only a single negative, because the stress
family has few same-family size-matched neighbors.

\subsection{Rewrite-step prediction}
The rewrite track is one immutable $6$-arm $\times$ $3$-seed grid: the same
goal-conditioned GNN over each of the four channels, one compute-matched prefix
transformer, and one uniform sample from the exact registry-derived legal-action
mask. We report demonstration-action top-$k$ match \emph{separately} from
exact-successor-structure top-$k$ match, together with the verifier-valid proposal
rate and every attempted/valid/invalid/unsupported/no-action/timeout/verifier-error
denominator. Semantic equivalence is never called demonstration correctness.

\subsection{Proof and simplification}
The proof track is bounded to exactly $256$ frozen proof problems; the
simplification track to exactly $1{,}000$ frozen v1 expression IDs. A proof counts
as successful only when the retained status is a verified terminal success, the
exact target structural signature is reached, the full transition trace replays,
the terminal claim is verifier-confirmed, and no invalid transition or verifier
error contaminates it; a lower cost, a matching string, or a producer's claim is
not proof success. Uniform, policy, policy-plus-value, and transformer methods
share one identical beam/expanded-node/generated-state/proof-depth/wall-time/%
verifier-call budget, and the primary paired contrast charges every unsuccessful
row at least its configured expanded-node budget so that a crash, timeout, or
failed proof can never look like an efficiency win. Simplification selects the
cheapest visited valid state under one preregistered Goal~3 exact cost
(\texttt{pure\_eml:official\_v4}) with a deterministic tie-break, and the
goal-conditioned witness-distance value is never used as an undeclared cost.

\subsection{Symbolic regression}
The SR benchmark is frozen: $256$ synthetic tasks plus a $32$-task restricted
Feynman-style subset~\cite{udrescu2020ai}, with a $100$-formula exclusion ledger
retaining every excluded case and verifier-confirmed recovery as the only recovery
criterion. The synthetic tasks were generated and determinism-verified on CPU
(byte-identical double builds), and the coordinator freeze recorded canonical
manifest checksums for the synthetic-$256$, development-$32$, and Feynman-$32$ sets
that reproduced identically across three independent CPU builds.\anchor{docs/specs/SR\_TASK\_SPEC.md
(coordinator freeze 2026-07-30); accepted Feynman count exactly $32$.} Production
compares an EML-guided arm, an AST-guided arm, a matched PySR or explicitly
labeled GP fallback (both implemented; neither ever relabeled as the other), and a
compact transformer-SR arm under frozen budgets and three seeds, reporting exact
and verifier-confirmed recovery over both attempted and verifier-supported
denominators, plus invalidity, timeout, and resource use, and the
accuracy--complexity Pareto front rather than best accuracy alone.

\subsection{Frontier-LLM panel}
An external, verifier-normalized reference panel is implemented but is
\emph{external context only}: no row it produces may enter the controlled gates
G6--G11. The frozen workload is exactly $100$ proof and $100$ SR tasks per model,
$200$ attempts per model, using the same two ordered ID lists (hashed and
persisted before any call) across four providers, for $800$ retained
success/failure rows when a model completes. Every provider fact --- exact model
ID and snapshot, access date, prompts and their hashes, reasoning settings, token
and time budgets, supported sampling fields, usage, latency, retries, cost, raw
responses, and verifier-confirmed correctness --- is recorded. No paid call has
been made; the panel ships with paid calls disabled and a zero cost guard, and
claimed correctness is kept distinct from verifier-confirmed correctness.

\subsection{Raw expansion is disqualifying (C2)}
Compiling all $250{,}000$ expressions with the pinned official-v4 compiler
succeeded on every row and produced a median tree expansion factor
$\alpha=40.6602$ over the source AST, with mean $952.1371$, standard deviation
$23{,}155.56$, p90 $385.07$, p99 $10{,}448.60$, and maximum
$6{,}481{,}679.31$.\anchor{docs/goals/goal2/GOAL2\_SUMMARY.md, ``Result'' and

mean/median gap records a heavy right tail --- dominated by the trig/hyperbolic
family (median $270.46$) --- not a typical $952$-fold expansion; we report the
full distribution in Appendix~\ref{app:dist}. Against the six preregistered
per-family thresholds $1+\ln K/\ln 4L$ (ranging $1.2914$--$1.5000$), and against a
whole-grammar counting break-even of $\approx\!1.56$, the strict pass count is
$0/250{,}000$: no expression in any family is even close to the regime where a
reduced operator vocabulary pays for its node count (Table~\ref{tab:families}).
These thresholds are descriptive combinatorial references, not empirical
performance laws.

\subsection{Exact compression is not competitiveness (C3)}
Lossless hash-consed sharing compresses expanded EML trees by a mean factor of
$39.3750$, yet the shared EML DAG remains larger than the \emph{uncompressed} AST
tree for every single one of the $250{,}000$ expressions; the best remaining ratio
is exactly $8/7$.\anchor{docs/goals/goal3/GOAL3\_SUMMARY.md, ``Structural
verdict''; FINAL\_REPORT.md \#c3-dag-sharing-and-graph-costs.} The most
compressible expression shrinks by $28{,}087{,}277/180$ and still ends $540/13$
times larger than its AST. Mean EML-DAG/AST-tree is $8.3344$ and mean
EML-DAG/AST-DAG is $10.4750$. Per-expression ratios are exact reduced rationals;
aggregate means use deterministic compensated \texttt{Decimal} accumulation in
authoritative corpus order and are labeled approximate. ``Compresses well'' and
``becomes structurally competitive'' are different claims, and only the first is
true here.

\subsection{Verified semantic rewriting helps a little, conditionally (C4)}
On a frozen, stratified $30{,}000$-expression selection, bounded equality
saturation with complete application provenance and independent candidate
verification improves $4{,}349/18{,}210$ costed rows ($23.882\%$) under
branch-insensitive real rewrites (\texttt{safe\_real}) and $5{,}026/18{,}210$
($27.600\%$) with guarded positive-real rules
(\texttt{positive\_real\_formal}), at mean relative savings of $2.361\%$ and
$2.849\%$ over costed rows and a largest single reduction of $302$ exact EML-DAG
nodes.\anchor{docs/goals/goal4/GOAL4\_SUMMARY.md, ``Production result'';
FINAL\_REPORT.md \#c4-e-graph-and-motif-compression.} Coverage is the binding
caveat: $11{,}566$ rows per mode ($38.553\%$) contain operators outside the closed
rewrite vocabulary --- including \emph{all} trig/hyperbolic and mixed-elementary
rows --- so these results hold on $60.700\%$ cost coverage and are conditional on

and zero degraded selections; the source form is always a costed safety anchor, so
improvement rates cannot be inflated by dropped rows. The formal library found
$677$ more improved rows than the safe library, concentrated in \texttt{exp\_log}
and \texttt{ood\_stress}.

\subsection{Two preregistered nulls (C4)}
Both Goal~5 comparisons were published in the frozen null-before-positive
order.\anchor{docs/goals/goal5/GOAL5\_SUMMARY.md, ``Learned and neural
comparisons''.} First, a learned motif vocabulary did \emph{not} beat the
equal-budget frequent-motif vocabulary on TEST\_IID dictionary-inclusive MDL:
$324{,}485{,}346$ vs.\ $317{,}678{,}264$ bits over $25{,}000$ expressions (it did

structural heuristic under the frozen comparison order: $8{,}349/18{,}050$
exact-best rankings vs.\ $8{,}796/18{,}050$ for the estimated-EML-tree-cost
heuristic (and $8{,}661/18{,}050$ for the AST-DAG-cost heuristic). Learned
components, so far, add nothing over honest structural baselines on these tasks.
This is the shape of finding the paper is built to surface: compression that does
not translate into competitiveness, and learned machinery that does not beat a
transparent rule.

\section{Limitations, integrity, and reproducibility}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{One scale.} Everything is measured at one fixed 250k-v1 scale; we fit
no scaling law and extrapolate to no other corpus, budget, or grammar. The
expressions are synthetic and generated, and the downstream benchmarks are
bounded, which limits external validity. With three seeds, planned learning
effects will be descriptive, not asymptotically significant.

\paragraph{Bounded semantic audit.} Numeric semantic verification covers a
deterministic sample, not the whole corpus. The original Goal~2 audit materialized
$273$ of $250{,}000$ rows ($203$ passed, $45$ nonfinite, $22$ overflow, $3$
retained mismatches; $7$ further selections exceeded the materialization
limit).\anchor{docs/goals/goal2/GOAL2\_SUMMARY.md, ``Semantic audit and retained
issues''.} A widened audit over the byte-identical pilot corpus processed a
further $2{,}988$ rows at ${\approx}0.085$\,s/row ($2{,}341$ passed, $607$
nonfinite, $37$ overflow, $3$ mismatches) before deterministically hanging on the
next row --- reproduced twice --- which is itself a correctness-surface finding: a
row whose numeric materialization exceeds practical time, motivating a per-row
timeout in the production audit tool. The combined evidence base is thus $3{,}261$
rows with $6$ retained mismatches (${\approx}0.1\%$), plus one documented
pathological-row hang. We claim structural determinism corpus-wide but numeric
verification only for the audited sample; no mismatch is relabeled or resampled
away.

\paragraph{Conditional coverage and equal-compute.} The e-graph results hold on
$60.7\%$ cost coverage; the excluded families include trig/hyperbolic, the family
with the worst expansion, so the semantic-compression numbers cannot be read as
corpus-wide. Because pure-EML expansion changes input size and compute, equal
parameters are not equal compute in the learning tracks, and we report both sides
rather than one.

\paragraph{Conformance, not capability.} Gate G10's published fail bounds the
grammar-v2 surface; it neither enables nor blocks any corpus or learning result,
and remains unresolved by design until a new recorded scope decision. The
frontier-LLM panel is external context: proprietary training data,
nondeterminism, availability, and model drift keep it out of every controlled
gate.

\paragraph{Artifacts and regeneration.} Production artifacts are stored
off-repository; the repository commits their SHA-256 digests, per-goal manifests,
and exact CPU reproduction commands, and every run installs from a single frozen
lock file (whose digest was re-frozen to the committed content after a disclosed
discrepancy); a CPU-only CI verifies both the floating resolve and the frozen lock
environment. Generation reproduces on commodity hardware: the development stage
($1{,}000$ rows) regenerated in $42$\,s, and the runner's own determinism gate
ran the $10{,}000$-row pilot twice and verified them byte-identical (corpus hash
\texttt{df4f3d74\ldots}, zero differences). The $250$k stage refuses machines
without a twofold memory margin: on a $16$\,GB laptop it projected a
$13{,}975{,}961{,}600$-byte peak, required $27.95$\,GB, found $3.3$\,GB available,
and declined to run degraded --- the apparatus enforces its own hardware floor
rather than trusting the operator. Certification is incomplete and recorded as
such: the binding machinery ran, authenticated zero artifacts in the archive's
absence, and committed a per-claim pre-authentication digest index ($27$ of $28$
manifest entries missing, $35$ contractual errors retained) instead of claiming
certification it lacks; the license was confirmed canonical MIT under a three-way
consistency check. We say so rather than claiming certification.

\section{Conclusion}

At the fixed 250k scale the compression premise fails quantitatively: median
$40.66\times$ expansion against per-family thresholds of at most $1.50$, zero AST
parity under exact sharing on any of $250{,}000$ expressions, marginal
coverage-conditional relief from verified rewriting, and learned components losing
both preregistered comparisons. None of this shows the representation is useless
for \emph{learning}; it shows that its structural cost is real and measured, so any
future learning benefit must pay for it. That question is open, the frozen
apparatus to answer it is in place --- with its pair pipeline already exercised on a
pilot corpus --- and the published record, two nulls and one gate fail, none
softened, is the standard any answer will be held to.
